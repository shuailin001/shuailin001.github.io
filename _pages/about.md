<p>I’m an algorithm engineer focused on applied AI, semantic retrieval, and data products. At Huawei’s AppGallery, I developed embedding-based search and query understanding methods using app metadata and user interaction data. My work connected representation design with relevance evaluation, production integration, and online experiments.</p>

<p>I hold a Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University, where I worked on representation learning and neural compression for time-series data. I’m interested in using AI to make complex data easier to retrieve, evaluate, and use, and in building reliable data products around those capabilities.</p>
